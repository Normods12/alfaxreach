1. Who we are

AlfaxAI ("AlfaxAI", "we", "us") provides B2B outreach, web and automation services. Our office is in LB Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. For anything related to this policy you can reach us on +91 99306 63231 (call or WhatsApp).

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry details: your name, company, phone/WhatsApp number, website, type of business and a description of your ideal client, when you fill in the form on our website.
  • Messages and calls: what you share with us by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • Client information: details we need to deliver our services, such as your offer, target market, approved email copy, calendar link and billing details.
Our website form does not store your details on our server. When you press submit, it opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

3. If you received an email from us

We send business-to-business emails on behalf of our clients to people whose role suggests our client's product or service may be relevant to them. For this we process business contact information only:

  • Name, job title and company
  • Work email address and, where available, business phone number
  • Publicly available professional information, such as a company website or professional profile

This information comes from publicly available sources and from B2B data providers. We do not use sensitive personal data, and we do not email personal or consumer addresses on purpose.

Every email we send names the sender and includes a simple way to opt out. If you opt out, we stop emailing you straight away (see section 10).

4. How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call
  • To provide our services to clients: researching leads, writing and sending emails, handling replies and booking meetings
  • To verify that email addresses are valid before sending
  • To send invoices and manage our client relationship
  • To honour opt-out requests and keep a suppression list, so we never email someone who asked us not to
  • To keep our website and systems secure, and to meet legal and tax obligations

We process personal data with your consent where you give it to us (for example, by contacting us), to perform a contract with our clients, to comply with the law, and, for B2B outreach, because sending relevant and easy-to-decline business communications is a reasonable business purpose. Where laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR (EU/UK) apply, we follow their requirements.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as website hosting, email sending, email verification, data providers, calendar and messaging tools. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Our clients: if you reply to an outreach email with interest, we pass your reply and contact details to the client on whose behalf we contacted you, so they can follow up.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to.

Some providers may store data outside India. When that happens, we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

6. Cookies & third-party services

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. Links to WhatsApp, phone and Google Maps take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become clients: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client records: for the length of our engagement, and afterwards as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes.
  • Outreach contact data: only for the duration of the client's campaign, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Opt-out list: we keep the minimum needed (your email address) for as long as we operate, so we can keep honouring your request.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including HTTPS on our website, restricted access to client and lead data, and reputable service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Correct or update inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Withdraw consent you've given, or object to us using your data for outreach
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights for you (under Indian law)

To make a request or raise a grievance, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

10. Opting out of our emails

If you no longer want to hear from us, you can:

  • Reply to any of our emails with "unsubscribe" or "not interested", or use the opt-out link in the email, or
  • Message or call us on WhatsApp / +91 99306 63231 with the email address to remove.

We act on opt-outs promptly, usually within 2 business days, and add you to a suppression list. This means you won't be contacted again by us for any of our clients.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy & how to contact us

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.
