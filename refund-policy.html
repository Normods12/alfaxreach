Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We keep this simple: no long-term contracts, cancel any time, and clear rules on refunds. If your written proposal with us says something different, the proposal applies.

1. Cancelling your plan

  • Our monthly plans have no long-term contract. You can cancel any time.
  • To avoid being charged for the next month, tell us before your next billing date by WhatsApp or phone.
  • After you cancel, your campaign keeps running until the end of the month you have already paid for. We will also pass you any interested replies received during that time.

2. When you can get a refund

  • Before work starts: if you cancel within 7 days of payment and we have not yet started work on your campaign, you get a full refund.
  • If we can't deliver: if we cancel the engagement or can't provide a service you have paid for, we will refund the unused part of your payment.
  • Payment mistakes: duplicate or incorrect charges are always refunded in full.

3. What is not refundable

  • Setup costs already spent, such as domains, mailboxes, data and tools bought for your campaign. Domains and mailboxes bought for you can be transferred to you instead.
  • Monthly fees for a month that has already started, including partly used months.
  • Refunds based on results. Because replies and meetings depend on factors outside our control, we do not refund on the basis of results, unless we have agreed a specific guarantee with you in writing.

4. How to request a refund

Message or call us on WhatsApp / +91 99306 63231 with your name, company and invoice number. We will reply within 2 business days.

Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 7–10 business days. How quickly it reaches your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

5. Digital services only

AlfaxAI provides digital and professional services. We do not ship physical products, so no shipping or delivery charges apply.
