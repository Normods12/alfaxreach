Our story

Great businesses shouldn't stay hidden.

We kept seeing the same thing. Many B2B companies had a strong product, skilled teams and happy clients. But their growth depended on referrals and luck. When referrals slowed down, so did the business.

The buyers were out there. They simply had never heard of these companies. Ads were expensive, and most cold outreach was so generic that people ignored it.

So we started AlfaxAI to fix that. We combine careful research, personal writing and smart automation to give every serious B2B business a steady, predictable way to reach the buyers who need them, without hiring a full sales team.

"Every message we send should feel like it was written by a person who actually cares, because it was."
